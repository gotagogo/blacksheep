The metaphor

Every flock needs one suspiciously creative sheep.

有害群之馬的隱喻

“Black sheep” is often used for someone who does not fit in. Here, we flip it: the outsider becomes the mascot of originality, humour, and tiny rebellion.

The “black seep” pun

A little black ink, a little sheep, a little mistake that becomes a character. The weirdness slowly seeps into the whole flock — and suddenly everything looks more alive.

Moral of the field

Being different is not always a defect. Sometimes it is the whole point. The black sheep is cute, funny, slightly naughty, but never boring.
