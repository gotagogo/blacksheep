The story

Once upon a perfectly white flock, one sheep leaked personality everywhere.

The metaphor

“Black sheep” can mean the troublemaker, the outsider, the 有害群之馬. Here, we flip it: the strange one is not the problem — the strange one is the plot.

“Black seep” is our visual pun: a little black ink, humour, and personality seeping through the bland surface.
